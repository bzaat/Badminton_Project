Architecture

One validated data container feeds three analysis tasks; raw video is used to check the annotations independently.

Pipeline

flowchart TD
    RAW["BFMD JSON: shuttle track, COCO-17 pose,
player boxes, hits, rallies, court"] --> ALIGN["Frame alignment
player_bbox +1, shuttle 0, pose 0"]
    ALIGN --> COURT["Court model + homography
pixels to metres"]
    COURT --> MD["MatchData, schema v5
329,819 frames, 1,058 rallies, 11,301 hits"]
    MD --> HIT["Task 2: hit detection
logistic regression"]
    MD --> MOVE["Task 4: movement in metres"]
    HIT --> SHOT["Task 3: shot type
gradient-boosted trees"]
    VIDEO["Raw match video"] --> T5["Task 5: TrackNetV3"]
    T5 -. cross-check .-> MD
    VIDEO --> LN["Label-noise kit"]
    LN -. timing check .-> HIT

Design decisions

DecisionEvidence
Player boxes shifted +1 frameIoU with pose boxes 0.97–1.00 shifted vs 0.88–0.91 unshifted, 12/12 matches
Court corners are the doubles outer linesNet-post bases project within 5 cm; singles hypothesis off by up to 47 cm
Ground point = ankles + 0.10 × box heightFitted on 6 matches; server asymmetry 0.01 m on the other 6 (was 1.6 m)
Shuttle = box (x, y) corner, not the README's centreTrackNetV3 on raw video: offset exactly −6.12 px before the fix, median 2.2 px after
Hyperparameters fixed in configNo tuning on test folds; model gap robust (paired CI +0.043 to +0.083 macro F1)

Engineering

  • About 5,400 lines of Python; 73 pytest tests with a synthetic BFMD fixture plus real-data tests.
  • One command (python -m badminton_analysis all) rebuilds everything in about 8 minutes on CPU; build peak 0.6 GB RAM.
  • Config-driven (configs/default.yaml); schema-versioned caches rebuild themselves when the format changes.
  • Colab notebooks for the GPU steps, tested cell by cell with nbclient; TrackNetV3 pinned to a commit with a SHA-256-checked checkpoint.

Trade-offs

  • Offline features use the whole rally: more accurate shot types, not real-time.
  • Annotations as inputs for Milestone 1: fast iteration, but annotation errors cap performance until the detector replaces them.
  • Interpretable models over end-to-end deep models: with 12 matches, measurable ablations mattered more than peak accuracy.
