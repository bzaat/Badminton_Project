Fixing the dataset's documentation

BFMD's README defines the shuttle centre as the box centre. Running TrackNetV3 on raw video showed a constant −6.12 px offset in both axes; at 10× zoom the box's corner sits on the shuttle. After the fix, the trackers agree to a median of 2.2 px, and in the detector's labels the share of shuttle boxes reaching IoU ≥ 0.5 with the independent tracker rose from 0.7% to 55%.
