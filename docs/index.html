What it does

When

Detects racket–shuttle contacts from shuttle acceleration peaks and wrist cues, with a logistic-regression scorer.

What

Classifies 12 stroke types from 37 features (rally context, shuttle flight, court position, pose) with gradient-boosted trees.

How

Maps both players into court metres with a homography and measures distance, speed, zones and recovery positions.

Why it's trustworthy

  • No leakage: each parameter is chosen on 11 matches and tested on the 12th; the 9 matches never inspected during design scored higher than the 3 used for it.
  • Independent checks: rules of the game (server position, standing still before the serve) and a second tracker run on raw video.
  • Problems reported, not hidden: the dataset README's shuttle-centre definition was wrong by 5.12 px; it was found, fixed and its effect measured.
  • Reproducible: one command rebuilds everything in about 8 minutes on CPU; 73 automated tests.

Limitations

12 matches, 9 players and one broadcast style. Milestone 1 uses annotations as inputs, so annotation errors cap performance. The object detector has no results yet, and the hit-label-noise measurement is waiting for human annotation.
