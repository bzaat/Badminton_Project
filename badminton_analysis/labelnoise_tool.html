Rule

Mark the frame where the racket strings touch the shuttle. If contact falls between two frames, mark the last frame before the shuttle leaves the racket in its new direction. Use Approx. when motion blur or occlusion hides the exact frame.

If the crop doesn't show the hitter (e.g. a side camera), press F for the full frame. If the hit isn't visible at all (close-up, replay, out of shot), press N.

The frame you open on is random; it says nothing about where contact is.

Keys

  • ← → step one frame
  • Space play / pause (loop)
  • F toggle crop / full frame
  • Enter mark contact, 1 sure, 2 approx.
  • N no hit, O outside window
  • ↓ next unlabelled, ↑ previous item
